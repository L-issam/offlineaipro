Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Offline AI Pro.

Does the app work offline?

After setup and model download, document processing and AI answers run on your phone without an internet connection. Internet is required for Google Play purchases and to download models or app updates.

Is my data really secure?

Your documents and prompts are processed on-device and are not sent to a cloud AI service. This website uses Google Analytics for site measurement; see our privacy policy for details.

What document formats are supported?

Offline AI Pro supports native and scanned PDFs, Word (.docx), Excel (.xlsx / .xls), and images. OCR extracts text from scans; results can vary with image quality and layout. PowerPoint, ODT and similar formats are not supported.

Is Offline AI Pro on iPhone?

iPhone support is coming soon. The app is available now on Android via Google Play. Advanced agent and multimodal features ship on Android first; the iOS port will follow the same private, on-device approach.

Is the app fast on my phone?

Gemma 4 and other on-device models are designed for mobile use. Response speed and memory use depend on your phone, the selected model and the document.

Can I choose different AI models?

Yes. Choose from Gemma 4 mobile models and other compact or larger local models, depending on your device and the balance of speed, capability and memory you need.

Can I customize AI generation settings?

Yes! Advanced users can fine-tune generation parameters including temperature, top-k, top-p, max tokens, and context window. The app also monitors RAM usage and prevents crashes by checking available memory before loading models.

Why uncensored AI models?

Because your AI shouldn't be your babysitter. Commercial AI models are formatted for consensus and blocked legitimate research questions. Our uncensored models give you intellectual freedom - you're the judge of what you want to learn. 100% private, 100% free.
